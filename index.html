⭐ THE DESTINATION • 01 01

Qissa Resto Cafe

Nilambur Road Dine-In • Takeaway • Delivery

The signature culinary landmark for comforting feasts & late-night cravings.

Taking the culinary journey to its pinnacle, Amal founded Qissa Resto Cafe on Nilambur Road. Offering a complete premium dining experience — from authentic sizzling loaded Rumali Shawarmas and golden crunchy Broasts to gourmet Smash Burgers, thick Faloodas, craft Shakes, and icy chilled Mojitos.

🌯 Rumali Shawarma 🍗 Crispy Broast 🍔 Smash Burgers 🍹 Mint Mojito 🍓 Royal Falooda

Youthful ambition and a genuine love for great hospitality.

At just 19 years old, Amal VP set out with a bold vision: to build vibrant spaces where people connect over authentic food, uplifting hospitality, and heartfelt conversations.

THE CHAI HUB • 03 03

Chaywala

Thuvvur Center Chai Spot

The bustling town favorite for steaming chai & crispy snacks.

Amal's flagship venture, Chaywala in Thuvvur Center, became a beloved community cornerstone — renowned for hot steaming kadak chai, fresh snacks, and warm daily gatherings.

🫖 Kadak Chai 🥧 Hot Snacks ☕ Special Tea
